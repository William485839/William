# PK Partners site (practice redesign)

Scope: the whole site (home, who we are + profiles, what we do, where we are, what we have done, join us, contact, privacy). Mode: Persuade for the home page; Read for the content pages; the deal record is Operate-like (filter and scan).

Audience and job: owners, boards and investors checking whether PK Partners is senior, independent and has done deals like theirs; then contacting a named person. Proof: the 42 real deals, the 12 real people, 4 offices. No invented quotes, titles or figures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The deal tombstone is the site's unit. The record speaks: every deal is set as a formal announcement, and the home page opens on them. Refuses the stock advisory-firm hero (city photo, slogan, three service cards, stat row).

OWN-WORLD: The financial-press announcement page. White newsprint ground, near-black ink, deal regions in a field of financial-paper salmon with tombstone slips one tone lighter. One ink-blue accent for actions and links. One typeface, Schibsted Grotesk (Nordic newspaper grotesk), large and tight for display, centered only inside tombstones. Square corners. No rules or outlines: tone steps separate surfaces.

STORY: The visitor sees in one screen what the firm is and that it has done real, large deals; believes the partners are senior and independent; then picks a person or office and emails or calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A salmon announcement wall across the full width.
